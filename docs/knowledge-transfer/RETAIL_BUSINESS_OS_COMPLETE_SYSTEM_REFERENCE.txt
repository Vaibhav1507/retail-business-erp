1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
-------------------
1.1 What it is
 Retail Business OS (RBOS) is a personal learning project: an India-first, multi-tenant,
 local-first retail operating system for small shops (source: docs/knowledge-transfer/system-overview.txt
 section 1; docs/00-guardrails.md). Priorities in order (guardrails section 1): learning sound
 engineering, solving real small-retail problems, correct accounting and inventory, strong tenant
 isolation, simple workflows, free/local-first, complete vertical slices over feature count.
1.2 Constraints (docs/00-guardrails.md)
 - Not a commercial SaaS commitment. Product owner has final authority.
 - No company (H&M) resources of any kind. Free/open-source only; no paid APIs; must work with AI disabled.
 - Synthetic data only unless separately authorised. No secrets in files.
 - Package feeds limited to nuget.org and npmjs (NuGet.Config, .npmrc verified).
1.3 Target users / business
 Small shop owners (owner-operated), electronics first (docs/product/electronics-validation-plan.md,
 web/e2e/demo-electronics.spec.ts). INR currency, Indian formatting (en-IN) in UI (Pages.tsx inr()).
1.4 Vision vs reality
 Vision (docs/product/*, ADR-001..024 all "Proposed"): multi-vertical, multilingual, GST, offline, mobile, AI, CA workspace.
 Reality (code): a single-owner, English-only web app with catalogue, opening stock, purchases, sales, returns,
 customer receipts, expenses, owner money, stock adjustments, period lock, basic reports, barcodes,
 optional serial/IMEI at sale, invoice-image extraction, OTP login. No GST engine, no roles beyond Owner,
 no offline, no multilingual, no AI. (Evidence: routes list in section 23; migrations in section 21.)

10. ONBOARDING
--------------
Journey: AccountAccess.tsx (register/login, OTP) -> POST /auth/register (closed by default after first user) ->
POST /auth/login -> /me returns tenantId=null -> BusinessSetup.tsx -> POST /bootstrap creates core.tenant,
tenant_membership (Owner, Active), business profile, default stock_location(s), (Endpoints.cs lines ~75-105; details

App.tsx gate: if !tenantId or !profileComplete the app renders BusinessSetup only.
Branch creation: not a step. Opening balances: via Stock > Add Starting Stock. Account setup: lazy.
Password policy (Program.cs): min length 12, lockout 5 attempts / 10 minutes, unique email.

11. PRODUCT CREATION AND OPENING STOCK
--------------------------------------
Create: Catalogue.tsx -> POST /products -> CatalogueService (duplicate key = normalised name+brand+model, catalog.norm_text; SKU from catalog.sku_counter; generic SKUs "INT-").
Import: Stock > Import from Excel -> POST /products/import/read (file reading via ProductFileReader, has HttpClient registered - external call mechanism NOT VERIFIED), /preview (writes nothing; test Preview_classifies_rows_and_writes_nothing), /commit (once per key via catalog.import_batch; max rows enforced; test Commit_requires_key_and_limits_rows).
Opening stock: App.tsx OpeningStock component -> POST /opening-stock with Idempotency-Key -> OpeningStockService: validates (OpeningStockPolicy.cs: max 200 lines, no duplicate products, qty>0, cost>0, date not > tomorrow) -> inserts opening_stock_document/lines, stock_movement, upserts stock_balance and item_cost (WAC), posts journal Dr 1400 Inventory / Cr 3900 Opening Balance Equity (CostAndJournal.OpeningStock), writes audit_event and idempotency_record in one transaction.
Limitations: no images, no OCR into catalogue (OCR-like extraction is for purchase invoices), no fuzzy product matching, no bulk edit.

12. PURCHASE-TO-PAY

Implemented: Supplier -> POST /purchases (multi-line, qty, unit cost) -> stock_movement in, WAC update, journal Dr 1400 / Cr 2000 Accounts Payable (ProcurementService; evidence doc) -> POST /supplier-payments (Cash/Bank/UPI) Dr 2000 / Cr 1000 Cash and Bank.
Not delivered (evidence doc): freight/other charges, tax status/GST on purchase, bill-vs-receive split, purchase reversal, payment allocation to documents. Purchases are paid-at-receipt? Partial payment at purchase time: NOT VERIFIED. Duplicate supplier-invoice number prevention: NOT VERIFIED (test names show only idempotency-key duplicates). Supplier advances: NOT FOUND.
Returns: POST /purchase-returns reduces stock and payable; blocked if stock already sold (Supplier_return_blocked_when_stock_already_sold).

13. SALE-TO-CASH
----------------
Implemented: GenerateInvoice.tsx (#/sell/bill) builds lines (product, qty, price, discount, optional serials) -> POST /sales with Idempotency-Key -> SalesService (SalesPosting.cs): SalesRules.TryValidateSale; row-locked stock deduction at WAC; reject negative stock; sale + sale_line inserts; journal: Dr Cash/Receivables, Dr COGS 5000; Cr Revenue 4000, Cr Inventory 1400; serial persistence in sale_line.serials (migration 018); warning-only detection of previously sold serials (product-owner decision during development).
Cash/UPI must be paid in full; Credit requires a named customer, partial payment allowed. Over-payment rejected.
Later: POST /customer-payments (collection), POST /sale-returns (restock, reduce balance, excess becomes cash refund).
NOT FOUND: exchange, cancellation, split tender, card, GST-bearing invoice calculation (beyond gst_rate column), controlled bill numbering (sale.number is an identity column, migration 008 + later column add; ADR-016 numbering not implemented).
Printing: browser print of InvoiceDocument via #/print/sale/{id}.
